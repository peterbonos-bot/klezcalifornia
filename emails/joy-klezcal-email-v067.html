The season continues with the adventurous klezmer of Veretski Pass and a special workshop with The Klezmatics. Bruce Bierman leads dance at both sessions, bringing musicians, singers, and dancers together for a shared celebration.

MONDAY, DECEMBER 28, 2026 · 6–9 PM

Learn with The Klezmatics

Spend an evening learning directly from The Klezmatics, with Jewish dance led by Bruce Bierman. Choose Yiddish song, percussion, instrumental music, or dance, then come together to share what you’ve learned and keep the dancing going.

About The Klezmatics: Formed in New York’s East Village in 1986, this Grammy-winning ensemble has helped bring klezmer and Yiddish song to audiences around the world. Their music draws on Eastern European Jewish roots while opening into jazz, gospel, punk, and other global sounds.

On tour: Celebrating 40 years, The Klezmatics also play The Freight December 29–30. Concert details →

SUNDAY, DECEMBER 13, 2026 · 2–5 PM

Veretski Pass & Bruce Bierman

Explore the expressive sounds and dance rhythms of klezmer with Veretski Pass, or step into Jewish dance with Bruce Bierman. After the workshops, everyone gathers to bring the music and movement together.

Cookie Segelstein (violin), Joshua Horowitz (accordion), and Stuart Brotman (bass) bring decades of international performing and teaching to their distinctive approach to klezmer. Their music connects Jewish repertoire with the sounds of the Carpathians, Romania, and the Ottoman world.
