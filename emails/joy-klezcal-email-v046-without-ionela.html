SUNDAY, NOVEMBER 15, 2026  |  2–5 PM

The Romanian/Klezmer Connection

Klezmer and Romanian folk music have long shared melodies, rhythms, and ornamentation, shaped by generations of musicians playing alongside and learning from one another. That exchange continues today, keeping both traditions vibrant and evolving. Join us for an afternoon of hands-on music, singing, and folk dance to explore these connections together.

With Peter Bonos, Kim Goldov, Aaron Kierbel, and Čardak dance leaders Janetta Redžić and Alexandru Sandrovschii.

Meet Your November Teachers

Peter Bonos

Trumpet · Orchestra Euphonos

Bay Area musician and bandleader specializing in klezmer, romanian and Balkan styles. Peter leads Orchestra Euphonos, performs with Fanfare Zambaleta and produces the Berkeley Balkan Bacchanal.

Kim Goldov

Violin

Visiting from Seattle, a longtime klezmer and Eastern European music performer, Kim plays with the Freylakh Klezmer Band and Talne Trio, bringing decades of ensemble experience to the workshop.

Aaron Kierbel

Percussion 

Aaron has played klezmer for over 20 years with bands such as the Gonifs and Kugelplex. Aaron currently plays with Orchestra Euphonos, Orchestra Gold and Wind, string and skin.. An experienced educator, Aaron’s participatory workshops through rhythm, movement, and play.

Janetta Redžić & Alexandru “Alex” Sandrovschii

Dance · Čardak

Founder of the pan-Balkan ensemble Čardak, Janetta teaches through connection and shared movement. She is joined by Alexandru Sandrovschii, a Moldovan-Jewish member of Čardak, to lead the dance workshop.
